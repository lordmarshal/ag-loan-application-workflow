## Release goals

| Release | Date | Goal | Features | KRs it moves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | April 7, 2025 | Farmers can start an application online and get the right checklist | F9, F1, F2 | KR2, KR3 |
| R2 | July 14, 2025 | Documents uploaded in one place, checked instantly, data flows to LOS | F3, F4, F5 | KR1, KR2, KR4, KR5 |
| R3 | October 20, 2025 | Everyone sees status; documents signed electronically | F6, F12, F7, F10 | KR1, KR3, KR6 |

R1 landed in the second half of the spring operating credit peak, so real volume tested it quickly. R2 was timed to be stable before the fall equipment peak starts in September.
