# Change Management and Training Plan
## Loan Application Workflow Domain

## Who is affected and how

| Group | Size | What changes for them | Main concern | Approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Relationship Managers | about 300 | No more emailed lists or retyping; new portal and dashboard | "Will this replace me or come between me and my customers?" | RM advisory group; RM champions in every region; message: *more time for advice* |
| Credit Adjudicators | 40 | Files arrive complete with completeness score | "Will I be pushed to decide faster?" | Credit co owns the rules; decision time measured on Credit's 4 days separately |
| Customer Service Centre agents | 55 | Can see application status directly | "Will I get more calls about the portal?" | Trained before R1; how to guide customers through the portal |
| Lending Operations | 70 | E-signature, data already validated | Learning new e-signature process | Hands on sessions before R3 |
| Farm customers | about 14,000 applicants a year | New way to apply and track | Trust and ease of use | Help text, videos in both languages, RM still available |

## Training plan

| Release | Audience | Format | Length | Completion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | RMs, Customer Service Centre | Live virtual session plus 5 minute video | 60 minutes | 97% |
| R2 | RMs, Credit, Lending Operations | Live session, sandbox practice | 90 minutes | 95% |
| R3 | All groups | Short video and quick reference card | 30 minutes | 96% |

All materials in English and French.

## Adoption measures

| Measure | Target by Q4 | Actual Q4 2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Applications started in the portal (online or RM assisted) | 75% | 89% |
| Customers who start online without RM help | 50% | 67% |
| RMs who used the portal in the last 30 days | 95% | 98% |
| Customer Service Centre agents using the status view | 90% | 100% |

## Resistance and how it was handled

* **RM pushback after R1** ("my customers want to email me"): the Domain Lead joined 6 regional RM calls, showed pilot data on time saved, and added RM assisted applications (LA07). RM portal use went from 52% in April to 89% by December.
* **Credit concern about rushed decisions:** decision time reporting split into "time waiting for documents" and "time with Credit," which showed Credit's time was never the problem.
