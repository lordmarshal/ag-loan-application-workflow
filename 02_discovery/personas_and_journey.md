# Personas and Customer Journey
## Loan Application Workflow

Based on 18 interviews (8 farm customers, 6 RMs, 2 credit adjudicators, 2 Customer Service Centre agents), December 2024. All personas are fictional composites.

## Personas

### Marc Bouchard, grain farmer, Manitoba
* 2,400 acres, runs the farm with his sister through a family corporation
* Needs an operating line every spring and replaces equipment every few years
* Prefers French; does paperwork at night after field work
* **Frustration:** "I never know what they want until they ask for it a third time."
* **Needs:** a clear checklist for his exact loan, upload from his phone, status without calling

### Aisha Mensah, young farmer, Saskatchewan
* First land purchase, 320 acres, starting out with help from her parents
* Comfortable online; less familiar with lending
* **Frustration:** "I didn't know if two weeks of silence meant yes or no."
* **Needs:** plain language explanations, progress updates, help understanding documents

### Tyler Kowalski, Relationship Manager, Alberta
* Manages about 140 farm customers across a large territory
* Spends evenings sorting emails and retyping applications
* **Frustration:** "I became an RM to advise farmers, not to be a data entry clerk."
* **Needs:** documents in one place, data that flows into the LOS, fewer callbacks

### Sandra Lee, Credit Adjudicator, Regina
* Reviews about 30 files a week
* **Frustration:** "Files arrive thin, so I send questions back and the clock keeps running."
* **Needs:** complete files with the right documents for the loan type, accurate data

## Journey map: Marc applying for an operating line (current state)

| Stage | What Marc does | What he thinks and feels | Pain point | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aware | Realizes he needs spring operating credit | "Same thing every year, should be easy" | | Reminder and pre filled renewal |
| Contact | Calls RM, leaves a message | Mildly annoyed waiting | | Start online any time |
| Gather documents | Gets a generic emailed list in English | Confused; unsure what applies to him | P1 | Loan specific checklist, in French |
| Submit | Emails some, mails tax returns | Unsure if everything arrived | P2 | One secure upload place with confirmation |
| Wait | Hears nothing for a week | Anxious; calls Customer Service Centre | P6 | Status tracker |
| Follow ups | Gets asked for items 3 times | Frustrated; feels disorganized | P3 | Completeness check before submission |
| Decision | Gets approval call from RM | Relieved | | |
| Sign | Drives 90 minutes to sign papers | "In seeding season, really?" | P7 | E-signature |

## Design principles that came out of discovery

1. **Ask once, for the right things.** Checklist adapts to loan type and farm structure.
2. **Show progress.** Customers and staff see the same status.
3. **Type it once.** Data entered by the customer flows to the LOS.
4. **Respect the season.** Mobile friendly, save and return later.
5. **Bilingual by default.**
