# Lessons Learned
## Loan Application Workflow Domain, 2025

Domain retrospective held December 10, 2025.

## Keep doing

| Lesson | Why it worked |
|---|---|
| Measure where time actually goes before choosing what to build | Showed 12 of 21 days were document chasing, so effort went to the right place and Credit was not blamed |
| Pick one north star metric everyone shares | "First time complete" united RMs, Credit and Compliance around the same goal |
| Pilot every release in a few offices | Caught the trade name problem (BR4) before it hit every RM |
| Read the audit log as a product signal | The override spike revealed the dealer quote problem |
| Bilingual and accessible in the Definition of Done | No release was ever held up waiting for French or accessibility fixes |

## Change next time

| Lesson | What happened | Next time |
|---|---|---|
| Involve RMs before the first design, not after | Early RM pushback after R1 slowed adoption | Co design with the RM advisory group from discovery |
| Bring Credit's queue time into scope when decision time is a KR | KR1 missed by one day because Credit's 4 days were out of scope | Agree with Credit up front how their part of the clock will be handled |
| Plan release dates around the farming calendar from day one | R2 had to move two weeks to avoid peak season | Map seeding, harvest and peak lending weeks into the roadmap at the start |

## Advice for the next Domain Lead

1. Spend your first month listening to RMs, customers and Credit before writing any stories.
2. Own the outcome, not the output. Shipping features is not success; moving the KRs is.
3. Say no clearly and explain why, with data.
4. Treat Compliance and Privacy as co designers. Guardrails built in early cost far less than fixes later.
