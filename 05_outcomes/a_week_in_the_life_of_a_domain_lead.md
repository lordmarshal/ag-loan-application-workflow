# A Week in the Life of a Domain Lead
## Loan Application Workflow

This is what a typical week looks like for the Domain Lead in this project, during the build of Release 3 (September 2025, fall peak season starting). It is meant to show what the job actually involves day to day.

## Monday: set direction for the week

| Time | Activity | What the Domain Lead does |
|---|---|---|
| 8:30 | Review the KPI dashboard | Checks last week's decision time, completeness rate, override rate and status calls. Notices overrides jumped to 6% in Alberta. |
| 9:30 | Product Owner sync | Aligns both POs on priorities. Squad B wants to start the RM dashboard early; Domain Lead agrees, since the e-signature approval is still waiting on Legal. |
| 11:00 | 1:1 with sponsor (VP Lending Operations) | Shares progress, flags the Alberta override spike, previews a decision needed at steering. |
| 14:00 | Investigate the override spike | Reads the override reasons in the audit log. Pattern: Alberta RMs overriding the equipment quote rule because dealers send quotes late. |

## Tuesday: listen to the business

| Time | Activity | What the Domain Lead does |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Call with 4 Alberta RMs | Confirms the dealer quote problem. Asks: would a "quote to follow" option with a 10 day follow up work? |
| 11:00 | Meeting with Credit Adjudication | Proposes the "quote to follow" change. Credit agrees if the quote is required before the decision, not before submission. |
| 13:30 | Write the story | Writes a new user story with acceptance criteria for Squad B's backlog and asks the PO to bring it to refinement. |
| 15:00 | Customer Service Centre floor visit | Sits with two agents for an hour, listens to status calls, notes the questions customers ask most. Input for the R3 status wording. |

## Wednesday: keep the delivery engine running

| Time | Activity | What the Domain Lead does |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Squad A backlog refinement | Clarifies status stage names with the UX designer so they match the words customers used on calls. Confirms French wording is booked. |
| 11:00 | Privacy Office check in | Walks through the text notification design; agrees what goes in the updated privacy impact assessment. |
| 14:00 | Dependency follow up | Chases Legal on the e-signature provider approval. Gets a date. Updates the risk log. |
| 15:30 | Prepare steering pack | Updates the KR table and drafts two decisions for the sponsor. |

## Thursday: show and tell

| Time | Activity | What the Domain Lead does |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | Joint sprint review | Both squads demo the status tracker and RM dashboard to the RM advisory group and Customer Service Centre lead. Domain Lead captures feedback and links it to the roadmap. |
| 13:00 | Regional lending managers call | Explains what R3 means for their teams in the fall peak and the extra support rota. |
| 15:00 | Pilot planning | Picks 5 pilot offices for R3, balancing regions, French and English, and digital comfort. |

## Friday: measure, document, think ahead

| Time | Activity | What the Domain Lead does |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Update process documentation | Updates the future state map and the RM procedure for "quote to follow." |
| 10:30 | 2026 discovery | Interviews a land appraiser and two RMs about land loan pain points for F8. |
| 13:00 | Weekly note to stakeholders | Short Confluence update: what shipped, what is next, what we learned. |
| 14:00 | Thinking time | Reviews the WSJF scores for next quarter with the latest data. |

## What the job really is

| The Domain Lead **is** | The Domain Lead **is not** |
|---|---|
| Accountable for business outcomes (the KRs) | The person who writes code |
| The owner of the end to end process | The person who approves credit |
| The bridge between the business, Credit, Compliance and IT | A project manager only tracking dates |
| The one who sets priorities and says "not now" | A Product Owner for just one squad |
| A translator of problems into clear, testable requirements | A note taker in other people's meetings |

## Skills used every week

* **Process thinking:** current and future state maps, finding where time is lost
* **Stakeholder management:** balancing RMs, Credit, Compliance and IT
* **Agile product practices:** backlog, user stories, acceptance criteria, WSJF, demos
* **Data:** reading KPIs, spotting patterns (like the override spike), measuring outcomes
* **Risk and compliance judgement:** knowing when privacy, KYC or credit policy must shape a design
* **Communication:** steering packs, weekly notes, explaining trade offs in plain language

## How a compliance and PMP background fits

| Your experience | Where it shows up in this role |
|---|---|
| Compliance and controls | Designing guardrails (identity checks, audit trail, override logging) into the workflow, and working credibly with Compliance and Privacy |
| Process mapping (Visio, draw.io) | Current and future state maps are a core Domain Lead deliverable |
| PMP | Release planning, dependencies, risk registers, stakeholder engagement, steering reporting |
| Banking and retail operations | Understanding frontline pain from the inside, like the RMs and Customer Service Centre agents |
| Jira and Confluence | Backlogs, stories, refinement and weekly updates live here |
